Case Study

Insurance Bar API — Multi-Role Insurance Management Platform

Comprehensive insurance management platform with role-based access control for admins, customers, partners, and partner associates. Features end-to-end policy management, claims processing, case handling, loan management, and support ticketing system.

Node.jsMongoDBPostgreSQLInsurance TechMulti-Role
Liveadmin.theinsurancebar.com

01 / The Challenge

Fragmented workflows across project stakeholders

Insurance companies and brokerages needed a unified platform to manage complex insurance operations involving multiple stakeholders (admins, customers, partners) with different access levels and requirements. The system needed to handle the complete lifecycle from policy creation to claims settlement while maintaining strict data security and role-based access controls.

02 / System Roles

5 distinct roles, one unified platform

🛡️
Super Admin
Platform-level control with full system access
👤
Admin
Manages customers, partners, policies, and system configuration
📋
Customer
End-users who purchase and manage insurance policies
📋
Partner
Affiliated agents who can create policies on behalf of customers
📋
Partner Associate
Team members working under partners with limited access

03 / Core Workflow

From project creation to delivery

01
User Onboarding & Role Assignment
Admins create user profiles and assign appropriate roles (customer, partner, partner associate)
02
Policy Creation & Management
Customers or partners create policies, attach partners, and manage policy lifecycle
03
Claims Processing
File claims against policies, assign administrators, and manage claim lifecycle
04
Case Management
Create cases linked to policies, progress through stages, and assign administrators
05
Loan & Subscription Management
Manage loans, approvals, and partner subscription benefits
06
Support & Resolution
Handle support tickets and resolve user issues
