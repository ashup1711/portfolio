Transportation & Freight Booking Platform

Mission-critical logistics platform with real-time IoT tracking, automated settlements, and complex role-based access control.

Case Study

Problem: Modernizing a legacy logistics process with real-time tracking and secure financial settlements across multiple stakeholders with varying access levels.

Solution: Modular monolith architecture with a robust Event Bus for internal communication. Integrated IoT APIs for live vehicle status and implemented a custom state machine for booking lifecycles.

Decisions: State Machine for booking status to prevent illegal transitions, Integration with SIM-based GPS for high reliability in remote areas, Delayed settlement logic to allow for dispute resolution

Results: Reduced manual coordination time by 40% and improved payment reconciliation accuracy by 98%.
